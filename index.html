About Me

I’m Mahdi. A computer engineer and a game designer who always been passionate about creating immersive and engaging gaming experiences. Everything about games fascinates me, from the art and storytelling to the mechanics and user experience. I love exploring new technologies and pushing the boundaries of what games can achieve. I believe that games have the power to inspire, educate, and connect people from all walks of life.

While I have a strong appreciation for 2D games especially pixel art I don’t limit myself to a single style or genre. I focus on solid game design, meaningful mechanics, and player experience first, adapting the visual style to serve the idea behind each project. I enjoy experimenting, learning, and evolving as a designer, with the goal of creating games that are both engaging and thoughtful.

PLANTS FOR PLANS

A simple focus game/application where you commit to a planned time session to help your plant grow to its full form.

You choose a focus duration, and start the time, then the work begins. The core idea is simple: the more you stay focused, the more your plant grows. Interrupting the session pauses or negatively affects the growth, encouraging consistency and self-discipline without pressure or punishment.

Each focus session plants a seed that slowly evolves through different growth stages, creating a calm visual reward for your effort. Over time, you build a small personal garden that reflects your dedication, patience, and progress. Every plant becomes a quiet record of time well spent.

The experience is designed as a cozy, distraction-free space, with soft visuals and gentle animations that help you put your phone away and focus on your work. The app stays quietly in the background, reminding you that meaningful growth takes time and care.

Contains

  • 2D pixel art
  • Random plants selection
  • Plants growing system
  • C# Scripting
  • Timer Selection Script
  • Simple UI/UX
  • Arabic Language Suppoort
